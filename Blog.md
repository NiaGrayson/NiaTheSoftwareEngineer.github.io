# Blog

The idea for this blog was inspired by the creation of my portfolio website.  I found myself knee-deep in building this website to display my work when I suddenly thought, “wait a minute. I have wanted a blog for years.” 

 I was so busy focusing on the way I wanted to present to a potential employer that I had completely overlooked the opportunity right in front of me. To create something for myself. Something that I am passionate about. Something I can build on my own terms. 

 If I am going to spend the time creating anyway, I may as well enjoy the process and live the dream while I’m doing it.  

At least, the dream of being a blogger. 😉
